# Pre-publication mathematical audit

Status: 28 October 2026; independent agent review of the local research dossier. This is not external peer review or formal verification. No claim of novelty is established.

## Claims checked

1. For fixed 0<α<1/2, with ordered PP and SS pairs whose factors exceed N^α, averaging SS−PP has coefficient [log²((1−α)/α)−1]/2. Its sign changes at α=1/(1+e). The moving-cutoff correction is lower order on macroscopic dyadic blocks, provided both PP and SS are separately defined. When α<1/3 higher-factor survivors occur; SS−PP is not the entire rough Liouville sum. Finite numerical findings do not independently prove infinitely many reversals.
2. The cubic rough signed identity T=SS−PP follows from reflection cancelling PS with SP; diagonal pairs must be counted once. A negative average does not imply pointwise negativity.
3. The 26.22% lower asymptotic proportion for the stronger sign condition uses Tolev's known mean-absolute Goldbach estimate, the rough-semiprime mean, a fixed finite residue-class Cauchy bound, then successive X→∞ and modulus→∞ limits. It is not a finite verified percentage and is not almost-all Goldbach. See https://www.impan.pl/shop/en/publication/transaction/download/product/83974 .
4. The complete-wheel average ratio 4e^(−2γ) follows from Mertens, averaging local factors, and prime-pair counts by PNT. Its conclusion is a no-go for one naive full-period main term; it is not a counterexample to Goldbach and not an actual pointwise estimate.
5. The rough triple-factor coefficient c3 is the classical Buchstab three-factor term: c3=(1/6)∫∫[uv(1−u−v)]^(−1)du dv over u,v≥α, u+v≤1−α. Its near-threshold expansion is 81/4·(1/3−α)²+O((1/3−α)^4). The X→∞ limit must be taken at fixed α before α→1/3. See https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2015/01/04/254a-supplement-4-probabilistic-models-and-heuristics-for-the-primes-optional/ .
6. The Chen identity R=(S−L)/2 is correct for q≥2 and Ω(q)≤2. A uniform positive fixed proportion for the unrestricted set is false by semiprime counting, while requiring rough complements changes the scaling. Landau semiprime asymptotic source https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.16491 .
