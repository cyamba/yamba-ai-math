# Keeping the parity target and source obligations explicit

29 October 2026. Root supplemental source audit,2 proxy units. This is an inspected-source report and research decision, not a proof of its claimed analytic transport.

Read the actual pinned [Guo LaTeX source](https://github.com/aster2024/openai-math-followups/blob/a605351dce1a55fb46a494d9cca9b0baa8f2734b/mobius-goldbach-shift/source/main.tex), including the input statements, labelled enlargement/intrinsic ratios, root count, TypeII transport and the classical-region obstruction. The earlier repository inspection had reached README level only. The source is materially closer prior work: it already uses labelled lattice-primitive enlargement and an ambient positive-power/restricted-root Gram argument. It explicitly says a full trace over added nonunit states is unnecessary for that route. An independent finite full-trace comparison can be useful, but must not be presented as the first enlargement method or as a necessary step.

The source's reflected parity claim is conditional on H2', correctness of specified LWW operator arguments, and a selected zero-free strip for primitive characters of conductors dividing kN. A stronger stated half-plane is offered as sufficient. Neither that half-plane nor the full H2' chain is validated here. On a small-radical class it instead cites classical zero-free and character-sum inputs, still conditional on H2'. Its TypeII section describes a transfer, rather than independently proving every load-bearing auxiliary-operator step.

The obstruction EC in that source assumes a separate configuration of split-prime scarcity. It is not a constructed counterexample among actual primes, nor is that configuration implied by merely postulating a zero close to1. Do not promote EC to a refutation of uniform parity cancellation.

The genuine intermediate analytic target is a bound for the actual-prime reflected correlation, such as sum over p<N of mu(N-p), with explicit uniformity and every analytic dependency proved or established. A new unconditional relevant theorem could constitute substantive progress; its novelty would need a closest-work and complete-proof audit. A theorem about unweighted roots, a source's conditional conclusion or a scalar padding identity is not that result. Even unweighted Mobius-prime cancellation at scale o(pi(N)) alone is not identified here with a Goldbach proof. The earlier endpoint-corrected signed-operator and polynomial-rough F margin remain open.

The original polynomial cutoff also remains a separate obstacle. Small required label groups vanish on that rough population; the inspected follow-up states only a subpolynomial roughness range. The present trace/root work does not fix the bounded-K or polynomial-label deficits. Keep the original Goldbach objective unchanged.

Next useful operator work should therefore prove or find a failure in the actual auxiliary edge/memory norm and moment chain, including its projections, prime-choice measures, distinctness and endpoint frame factors. It should then test the reflected arithmetic transfer and major part. Further unweighted reductions without such a connection must not be treated as completion.
