29 October2026. Root supplemental source access,2 proxy units. The author's public [preliminary book PDF](https://www.dms.umontreal.ca/~koukoulo/documents/publications/primes.pdf), D. Koukoulopoulos, *The Distribution of Prime Numbers*, Chapter26, printed page277, equation26.1, was downloaded, rendered and visually inspected. For fixed A>=0 it gives

\[
 \sum_{q\le Q}\max_{y\le x}\max_{(a,q)=1}
 |\pi(y;q,a)-\operatorname{li}(y)/\varphi(q)|
 \ll_A x(\log x)^{-A-1},\qquad
 Q\le\sqrt x(\log x)^{-A-3}.
\]

Assurance is this exact statement inspection, not a new proof or a full book audit. Endpoint differences are allowed.
