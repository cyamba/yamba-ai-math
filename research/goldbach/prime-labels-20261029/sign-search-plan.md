# Supplemental falsifier search for the original signed target

Registered before execution, 29 October 2026. The original cubic target is T(N)=SS(N)-PP(N)<0 for sufficiently large even N. Its eventual version cannot be refuted by one finite exception. A concrete nonnegative instance could nevertheless expose a missing threshold, suggest a structural family, and change the next proof attempt.

Screen every even N from 6 through 100,000, using prime and semiprime indicator convolutions at each cube-root prime threshold. NumPy from the already bundled runtime is permitted; no dependency installation or paid provider is involved. This is a floating-point candidate search. Check its small-range values by independent direct trial factorization, report maximum rounding residuals, and directly recount every nonnegative candidate with exact integer arithmetic. Do not label the absence of screened candidates an infinite theorem or an exact all-range proof.

There is one primary comparison: the screened statistic against exact direct values. The output preserves numerical candidates, their exact confirmations or rejections, range, implementation hash, runtime and limitations. Stop at 100,000; do not enlarge the scan merely because no candidate appears. This is supplemental to the analytic proof attempts, not a replacement for them.
