29 October 2026. Root agent acting as Literature Scout. Inspected the primary [Lue-Wang-Wu manuscript, arXiv:2610.20242v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.20242v1), dated 14 October 2026, specifically Theorem 1, equations (2.12)-(2.16), the statements of Propositions 4.2 and 4.5, and moment-transfer step (4.68). This is not a complete audit of the manuscript or its analytic source chain; its claimed theorem is unverified as a dependency here.

The auxiliary groups use exp((log x)^a)<p<=exp(2(log x)^a), with fixed exponents 0.1<a<0.2. The weight is a product of omega_i(n)*rho^(omega_i(n)-1)/V_i, where omega_i counts distinct group-prime divisors, 0<rho<1 and V_i is the reciprocal-prime mass. Proposition 2.3 states a mean-square approximation along p+2 after averaging disjoint collections. All group counts and exponents are fixed before x tends to infinity. The theorem statement is for fixed shift 2, not a moving Goldbach target. The Type II argument chooses a sufficiently large fixed group count for its requested precision and uses growing auxiliary lists and moments. These are source descriptions, not verified estimates for the present target.

Two exact support observations require independent proof/review in this tranche. Original group primes lie below x^alpha eventually and cannot divide an x^alpha-rough complement. Moving K disjoint required group divisors above x^alpha forces their product above x^(K*alpha), which cannot fit below a fixed multiple of x once K*alpha>1. Neither observation excludes a genuinely modified bounded-label or padding argument.
