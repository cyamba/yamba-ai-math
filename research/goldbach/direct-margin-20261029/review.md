## Complete checks of the load-bearing deductions

The cutoff excludes the prime 2, so the modulus has no nonunit survivors. H/2 is even and excluded. There is no missing unit, endpoint, square or midpoint correction.

For any A in these unit representatives, expanding the character square gives

\[
 h^{-1}\sum_\chi\chi(-1)|A_\chi|^2
 =\sum_{a,b\in A}1_{-ab^{-1}\equiv1\pmod H}.
\]

The congruence is a+b=0 modulo H. Since 0<a,b<H, it is exactly a+b=H. These are ordered counts. Mixed P,S and S,P pairs cancel under reflection because their Liouville signs are opposite. Consequently T=SS-PP=-D_H+R_H, with the displayed principal sign and normalization.

The exact intended convolution is

\[

Distinct-factor terms have two ordered representations and squares have one, explaining the final half-square term. The coupled product cutoff prevents replacing this with a product of two independent full-prime measures.

**Proved relative to ordinary PNT.** Exact least-factor enumeration is

\[

\[

\]

To justify the error, write pi(x)-li(x)=o(x/log x), uniformly for x>=z. Its two boundary products with f are o(1/L^2); its derivative integral is o(1) times O(1/L). Changing a strict endpoint costs at most O(1/(zL))=o(1/L). With t=log x/L, the integral is L^(-1) times the integral of 1/[t(1-t)] from log z/L to 1/2. Its limiting constant is log 2. Thus

\[
 p_0\sim H/L,\qquad s_0\sim(\log2)H/L,\qquad
 D_H\sim2(1-(\log2)^2)H/L^2.
