# Prior results and failures carried forward

This is a provenance summary, not a new proof or assurance upgrade. Read the
linked full arguments and verdicts before relying on a premise.

- [RR007 result](../rogers-ramanujan/rr007/result.md) and its
  [final KR review](../rogers-ramanujan/rr007/fleet-a/review-2.md) accept the
  restricted paper algorithms H/H1 on `a<=2` with arbitrary riggings and every
  `a=3,b=0`. H1 preserves A3 and the corrected b=1 operation. Their intrinsic
  alternating-path inverse may reject a target-only component; the image is
  not simply a union of auxiliary images. The common-mu operation W solves the
  all-b two-single obligation. These results do not cover a3,b>=1 or a>=4.
- The same reviewed revision accepts an exact full C-only triadic grammar.
  No canonical U cut/gluing matching it has been proved. Three Ferrers sheets
  encode U losslessly, but their total areas do not recover the object.
- The original external a3 formula overlaps the old a<=2 image beginning at
  weight36. RR007 repairs it by minimum fusion. Do not silently replace a
  failed candidate's text with the repair, or claim a different six-dimensional
  repair is justified by the prior review.
- In the fixed three-sheet embedding, a weight13 wall obstructs an
  A3-compatible pure two-cube-addition operation. It does not exclude slides,
  deletion or a different boundary encoding.
- A3 and the alternative B2 map assign overlapping objects differently. The
  old P/Q cost-eight union also collides. Source priority does not repair an
  overlap; a coherent extension needs an actual disjointness/splicing argument.
- Literal distinctness on A, B and U, and repeated entries in C riggings are
  different predicates. Counts from incompatible partial maps cannot be added.
- The old endpoint implementations received separate ordinary semantic reviews
  during RR007, but source-move attribution and formal certification remain
  separate. KR1 is not solved by coefficient counts or endpoint tests alone.
- The tableau total-order obstruction rules out an isolated-sector injection;
  determinant sectors must interact. A global used-set or exhaustive matching
  can establish a bounded computational correspondence without supplying a
  local structural cancellation theorem.
