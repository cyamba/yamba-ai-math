See `notebook.md` for the preserved pre-pilot definitions. Its initial snapshot
is intentionally unchanged; this is the subsequent research log.

1. H1 was implemented from the reviewed paper, using canonical guarded A3
   formulas. It passed exact image and both inverse checks through12,30,40.
   This adds executable assurance to a previously paper-only restricted result.
2. The proposed Phase 2 external tables fail at27–32. Their three structural
   losses are real: continuous parameters merged, branches discard increments,
   and the five-single fallback has the wrong weight. Preserve exact witnesses
   in result files, rather than correcting a finite sample case by case.
3. A simple infinite a4 plane survives: common mu and arbitrary final gap encode
   by repeated8s and an optional12 marker. An a5 analogue uses15/17 parity heads
   and repeated10s, with a mandatory terminal one. Exact languages avoid both
   old auxiliary and retained maps, so H1 can be extended coherently.
4. A systematic bounded-digit carrier covers *every* mu/nu at a3,b>=49;
   a4,b>=65; a5,b>=79. Quotient coin multiplicities store unbounded gap values,
   binary marker presence stores only bounded residues, full D_b retains all
   ordered nu information, and mandatory ones pay the finite marker overhead.
   This is an arithmetic coding theorem, not evidence that the geometric seam
   or scale-three U recursion has been solved. Large thresholds are conservative;
   the formulas were not instantiated above40.
5. The uniform base identity for pair-fusion is Q(a,b)-Q(a-2,b+1)=a-1.
   a3/a4 reductions are exact; a5 needs its weight4 digit split modulo3 and a
   child first mu divisible by4. This identifies a real boundary phase, rather
   than extending a weight-two operation without storing enough information.
6. New target-only clue: H2 still rejects(10,8) at18. Its g3 inverse is a3,b0
   with mu=(1,4,4), whose retained f1 image is(12,6). The latter is outside g,
   so this is a rejected target-only component, not an unexplained image hole.
   The same support pattern with free ones may encode the next a4 boundary,
   but no extra rule is retained or executed in this packet.
7. Possible small-b funding: replace D_b by a fixed dense larger contour and
   store b intrinsically in guard multiplicity; a sufficiently large final mu
   gap pays the extra finite volume. Other large gap digits might fund it too.
   This remains an unexecuted design clue; no cofinite-domain or finite-box
   theorem is claimed and no hidden ranked matching is allowed.
