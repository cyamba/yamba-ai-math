## Findings

1. **General theorem — supported.** For d,c>0, positive complementary exponents 0<r_i<c and distinct nodes, Xia v1 Lemma 2.2 gives the cited positive cut expansion. Sorting a layer by index and α before β on ties yields a weakly increasing word of cut and particle indices; precisely a β→α adjacent pair of particle letters forces strict increase. The converse reconstructs each inclusive interval, including repeated equal cuts and cells on both sides of a cut. Each gap coordinate δ_t contributes to exactly L−t+1 indices, giving denominator factors `(1−q^(dh))^−1`, h=1,...,L, and descent term `d comaj(w)`. Since m≤n≤L, each of the m marker weights has a distinct matching coordinate. The least-active toggle is a sign-reversing, weight-preserving involution; its fixed points have no markers and the last m coordinates zero. Summing free coordinates gives precisely the displayed denominator from h=m+1 through L. For fixed n and total weight, positive particle bases bound word length, and cut/particle indices and signed markers are bounded, justifying formal coefficientwise cancellation.

2. **Forbidden-cell corollary and empty word — supported with clarification.** Multiplication by `(1−q^r0)` cancels the α_0(0) geometric factor for every cut tuple. The intended formula already gives the correct empty-word contribution 1; the proof's sentence “use δ1=...” needs this explicit qualification.
