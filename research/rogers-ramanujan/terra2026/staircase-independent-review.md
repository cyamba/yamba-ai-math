## Flattening and cancellation

Given a cut object, the word lists each layer's particles sorted by increasing index and α before β at the same index, with cut symbols between layers. Inclusive endpoint factors mean particles of several adjacent layers may share an index; the cut symbols preserve their distinct layers. Every index in a lower layer is at most its right cut, and every index in the next layer is at least that cut. Therefore all flattened indices are weakly increasing. Within a layer, the only equality forbidden by the chosen color sort is an adjacent β followed by α; their indices must increase strictly. Conversely, any word with exactly n ordered cut symbols and any weakly increasing index sequence satisfying those strict descents can be uniquely reconstructed: the counts of preceding cuts define each layer, and monotonicity enforces both inclusive cut bounds. This includes repeated cuts and zero particles.

Subtracting the forced descents from the index increments produces nonnegative deltas and reversibly recovers all indices. Each delta at position t contributes to the indices of all positions t through N, hence weight `9(N−t+1)`. Forced descents and intrinsic particle bases depend only on the word, so changing deltas leaves them unchanged. Since N≥n≥floor(n/3)=m, the last m deltas exist. A marker h and delta at position N−h+1 both carry weight 9h. Toggling the least h with a marker or positive delta preserves its eligibility and all earlier ineligibilities, so the same h is selected on the next application; the operation is an involution, reverses sign, and preserves weight. Its fixed points are exactly no markers and those final m deltas zero. For n=0, m=0 and the empty-word case requires no toggle.

For A_n, `(1−q^(3n−1))` cancels the geometric factor for α at layer 0, index 0, which exists for every weak cut sequence. With n≥1, the first word symbol exists. Such a forbidden α at index 0 necessarily appears before all other layer-0 particles by the chosen sort and before the first cut, hence is the first symbol with index zero. The converse is immediate. Thus `j₁≥1` exactly when the first symbol is α encodes the forbidden cell. Subtracting this word-dependent initial offset changes only the fixed staircase term; the same marker toggle remains valid. The separate definition A_0=P1 is positive, and B_n is the labeled disjoint union of the C_n model and a copy of A_(n+1) shifted by 3n+2.

For fixed n and total weight, finitely many particles and common-tail parts are possible because each species has positive weight; every cut and index increment has positive weight, so there are finitely many choices. The cancellation is consequently valid coefficient by coefficient and uses no weight-wise rank matching. It says nothing by itself about bijections for the three-state recurrence, about a structural correspondence to the gap class, or about the full KR1 bijection.
