# KR1 investigation: failure, working-result, and open-obligation ledger

This ledger records the precise architecture ruled out by each witness. **FAILED** means that proposal is refuted within the stated assumptions, not that a broader approach is impossible. **WORKS** identifies a scoped mathematical construction or result, not a complete KR1 solution. **OPEN** means no total constructive proof has been supplied. Novelty is unverified. Finite check counts are supplementary evidence, not universal proofs.

## FAILED: carry and local repair proposals

- **Uncolored power carrying as a normalization:** replacing eight 1s by 8 identifies distinct legal sources `1^8` and `(8)`. Likewise `3^2` and `(6)` collide under the corresponding base-2 move. A bounded-digit family is a genuine source subset, not a normal form for all source partitions. Live alternatives include marked origins, different species, and coordinated global carries. Source: `construction/lane_carries.md`.
- **Unchanged-context proportional repair of a pair `(4s,4s)`**: `(s,7s)` collides on `10^8` versus `(10,35,35)`; `(2s,6s)` on `1^8` versus `(1,1,6)`; `(3s,5s)` on `8^8` versus `(10,10,10,10,24)`. Leaving `(4s,4s)` fails gap validity for 1^8. Even a replacement valid in isolation may fail in context: `(3s,5s)` sends `1^10` to `(2,3,5)`, where 2 and 3 violate the close-pair rule. Live: origin information, non-proportional moves, larger rearrangements, context-dependent rerouting. Source: `construction/lane_carries.md`.
- **Two-species, weight-preserving normalized carry injection:** `(r,0)` and `(0,1)` both have weight rs but there is only one output at that weight with the lower multiplicity <r. Live: borrowing other species, labels, or history. Source: `construction/lane_carries.md`.

## FAILED: finite-state and block simplifications

- **Fixed finite apex lookup as a global U–C bridge:** the earlier table has two distinct cluster inputs mapping to U=`(19,12,6,3,1)` at weight 41. Adding finitely many entries does not prove global injectivity. Live: unbounded coordinates and a genuinely invertible insertion algorithm. Historical provenance: `rr003`/PR 15; see paper baseline.
- **Independent five-height masks:** masks `{1}` then `{5}` encode heights 1 and 10, a forbidden gap 9, although each block is nonempty. Live: maintain prefix/suffix boundary state. Source: `construction/block_reduction.md`.
- **Forbid final mask `{1}`:** the one-height object `(1,0,0,0,0)` is valid; trailing zeros after the last occupied height are irrelevant. Live: explicitly distinguish the terminal block. Source: `construction/block_reduction.md`.
- **Rigid root-to-no-height-1 rule:** the weight-1 cluster root `(a,b,mu,nu)=(1,0,(0),())` cannot map to a weight-1 U without height 1. Live: charge-changing or non-root-preserving encoding. Source: `construction/block_reduction.md`.
- **Preserving the largest part or every fixed prefix:** weight-2 source `(1,1)` must go to gap object `(2)`; their largest parts differ. Live: allow global part expansion. The bounded-digit entropy obstruction strengthens this: no injection can have `max(output)≤γ max(input)+K` for fixed finite K and `γ<log(256)/log(159)≈1.09396`. It does not exclude larger expansion, unbounded carry, or global maps. Source and separate audit: `theory/local_surgery.md`, `entropy-independent-review.md`.

## FAILED: signed-cut and sequential-marker recipes

- **Assign a positive class independently to each fixed C_n cut:** for every n≥3 the zero-cut signed generating summand has coefficient −1 at q⁹. Other cuts can compensate. A naïve last-cut increase also removes occupied boundary cells: for C3 at zero cuts, a negative q⁹ marker plus a layer-3 particle 14 cannot simply retain the particle after the last cut moves. Live: move cuts and occupied particles jointly, or use the all-marker word coordinates below. Source: `theory/local_surgery.md`.
- **Terminal particle bump without the inverse color tie condition:** with unique α at terminal index 1 and β at index 0, moving α back yields a source where the forward tie rule would instead move β. Live: impose the exact inverse test, as in `construction/terminal_bump_review.md`.
- **Sequential q⁹ then q¹⁸ elimination by the same independent terminal shift:** at n=6, a surviving q¹⁸-marked state with zero cuts and one terminal index-0 particle becomes a prior q⁹ image if that particle moves forward by two. Live: the simultaneous least-eligible staircase involution works. Source: `theory/local_surgery.md`.
- **Dropping m≤n in the general positivity theorem:** for n=0,m=1,d=1,c=5,r₀=2, the full normalized product `(1−q)/((q²;q)_∞(q³;q)_∞)` has coefficient −1 at q. This does not exclude special parameters with m>n. The paper has the full product; an older theory note had a truncated display, with the same coefficient conclusion. Source: manuscript and `theory/general_positive_divided_differences.md`.

## WORKS: scoped constructions and proofs

- **General positive cut-word model:** for positive complementary monomial nodes, 0≤m≤n, the joint least-active marker/word-gap involution gives an explicit nonnegative normalized divided-difference model. Extra `(1−q^r₀)` forbids one initial cell. Specialization yields positive colored models for all auxiliary A_n,B_n,C_n; no recurrence bijections follow. The generic toggle is Lean checked; source expansion and flattening are hand-reviewed. Sources: manuscript, `theory/general_positive_divided_differences.md`, `staircase-independent-review.md`.
- **One q⁹ terminal cancellation:** the tie-corrected two-branch involution pairs every marked object. Alone it gives C₃,C₄,C₅ positive models; higher n retains other signed markers. The joint staircase construction supersedes the marker-interaction limitation. Source: `construction/terminal_bump_review.md`.
- **Two-bit residue lanes and paired swaps:** Euclidean digits split ±1 into uniquely invertible ±1, ±2 and ±4 lanes; ±3 is unchanged. The paired map `(rq+d,m)↦(rm+d,q)` is a weight-preserving involution on disjoint source chains, but does not generally normalize. Source and audit: `construction/lane_carries.md`, `local-constructions-review.md`.
- **Local bidegree surgery:** a fixed parity progression bijects one chosen triple of multiplicities with a tagged disjoint output union, preserving both exponents; labels and shared-species context are essential. It is not an all-P2-to-P3/K global schedule. Source and audit: `theory/local_surgery.md`, `local-constructions-review.md`.
- **Gap-to-cluster merge endpoint:** the direct pair/single merge and extraction have a universal author proof plus independent semantic review; boundary arithmetic and nonadjacent choice inequalities are partly Lean checked. It does not supply the product-to-cluster bridge. Source: `rr003/endpoint-cluster-merge.md`, `merge-independent-review.md`.
- **Intrinsic height blocks and gap automaton:** boundary-state conditions give an invertible height-block representation; the seven gap patterns and their transition table give a complete gap-side model, with local constraints checked in Lean. These are representations, not a bijection between the two classes. Sources: `construction/block_reduction.md`, `formal-review.md`.
- **Restricted no-3 branch at n=0:** P3 objects without literal part 3 embed into A₁ by a zero cut and layer/color exchange, with a recognizable inverse image. This is only a branch and coincides with an existing RR003 subcase; no novelty is claimed. Source: `theory/recurrence-experiments.md`.

## OPEN: decisive constructive obligations

1. Construct total, explicitly invertible and weight-preserving product-side maps realizing every branch of the C_n recurrence, with branch labels recoverable and survivors valid. The weight-4, 8, 27/36, and 37 examples are mandatory regression cases for any claimed architecture.
2. Supply analogous maps for the other two matrix rows and a proof that recursion terminates at the empty boundary for every finite object, including the n mod 3 phase when the locked-gap count changes.
3. Compose those maps with the literal gap-side branch decomposition, prove both global inverses and exact weight for all partitions, and provide executable endpoints with independent semantic review.
4. Alternatively construct a direct unbounded U–C or product–cluster map meeting the same requirements. No such global map is established here.
5. Formalize the source divided-difference expansion, cut flattening, full merge and any new global map if a complete Lean certificate is sought. Current Lean scope is listed in `formal/verification-scope.json`.
6. Check prior literature before any novelty claim. No finite enumeration, coefficient agreement, or series identity by itself supplies a structural bijection.
