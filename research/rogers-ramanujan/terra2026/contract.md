## Proof obligations

For product partitions B with residues 1,3,6,8 modulo 9 and gap partitions A with distance-two gaps at least 3 and close-pair sums divisible by 3, provide maps B to A and A to B, prove validity, equal weight, termination and both inverse identities. A larger theorem qualifies only if its explicit specialization supplies these maps.

Geometric encodings and automata covering all A or all U are useful global reductions, but they do not close the B-to-A bridge. The known N=41 collision and the fixed finite alphabet impossibility remain mandatory regression evidence.
