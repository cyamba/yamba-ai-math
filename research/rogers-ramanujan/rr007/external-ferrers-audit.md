## A useful exact recursive identity, with its missing interface

Writing a=3c+r,0<=r<3 gives the algebraic identity

`Q(3c+r,b)=r²+3r(b+2c)+3Q(b,c)`.

The rigging denominators admit a corresponding positive factorization:

`(q^9;q^9)_c/(q;q)_(3c+r)`

`= product_(j=1..c)(1+q^(3j)+q^(6j))`

`  / product_(1<=i<=3c+r,3 does not divide i)(1-q^i)`.

This follows by cancelling each(1-q^(9j)) against(1-q^(3j)). It is an
exact formal-series identity. Thus a recursion must transport unrestricted
nonmultiples-of-three boundary parts and ternary digits for the3j parts,
as well as the scaled inner C object and the r-dependent base offset.

image nor a unique recursive cut. These obligations are passed to the
