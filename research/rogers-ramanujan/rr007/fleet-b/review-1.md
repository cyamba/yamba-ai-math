Independently: for h=5k+r and h'=5l+s, the condition 0<=h-h'<=4 is
equivalent to k-l=e(r,s). Unfolding a secondary into
(k+e(i,j),i),(k,j) supplies that equality internally. For consecutive
secondaries with lower primary sizes k,q and colors ij,lm, the F2 equation

unfolding any F2 gives a primary
flat sequence. Its final size is zero and its successive size drops are

the required drops.

left. It has the exact required F2 image. Unfolding and removing the zero

## Primary reinspection and the weight-eleven contradiction

Only HTML was inspected; no PDF, TeX, erratum or author confirmation was
retrieved. This establishes the displayed interface, not a verified

Here is the independent mathematical contradiction under that literal
interface. Denote the nonground color labelled 1 by x, to distinguish it
from the formal unit. Decoded heights (5,1,1), of literal U weight eleven,
give F2=(1_0x,0_x0,0_00). The F2 adjacent E values are one and zero, so
this is valid, has raw total one, and has nonground word xx.

Every R2 object with that raw total and word has either a single xx atom
or two one-letter atoms. The first option is impossible since xx has even
raw parity. Nonterminal 00 is prohibited, and all sizes are nonnegative,
so the second option forces exactly 1_0x and 0_x0. In that order the
printed threshold is three while the difference is one. In reverse order
the threshold is one while the difference is minus one. No printed-R2
object can meet those invariants. This is an exhaustive symbolic
nonexistence proof, independent of coefficient counts or the section-five
algorithm. In R2star the first threshold is one, and it is exactly the
image of the valid M sequence P(1,x),P(0,x). Thus the discrepancy is
material and B3's distinction is necessary.
