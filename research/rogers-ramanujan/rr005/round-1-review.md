2. **The universal chain arguments are supported.** For U, removing a sole 1 fails exactly when the next height is 5. The second triple image has a unique height-2 column, with every other height at least 5 and at least one height 6; hence removing the 3 fails its terminal test, separating it from the append-3 image. For C, the R1 images are separated by a strictly excessive last mu, a=1 with mu=0, and a=2 with mu=00. The R3 images are separated by the exceptional (0,1,(),(0,)), strict final nu, a=3 with first mu zero, and the strict boundary before the last three mu entries. The displayed inverses recover every coordinate. Domain validity, exact weight changes, and the stated D classifications follow in all boundary cases. A deterministic lowering predecessor and a strictly decreasing nonnegative weight establish global unique normal forms; the argument does not depend on finite agreement.

3. **The two-root result has the stated exact scope.** Both proposed base roots are E roots of weights 0 and 6. Repeated U triple raising adds only threes on those chains, and unit raising adds only ones, so the exact U source is the stated union: ones/threes, or ones/threes with exactly one six. Normal forms separate the chains and prove both restricted inverse identities. The formulas for `(6,3^t,1^s)` follow from the actual R3/R1 branches, including t=0, t=1 and s=0. This partial map need not agree with older partial assignments, and its proof does not inherit any earlier unreviewed endpoint claim.

4. **The complexity bound is defensible only for the supplied reductions and partial maps.** For total input/output weight N, each valid U has at most N columns and each coordinate has O(log(N+2)) bits; C has at most O(N) stored coordinates under its positive quadratic base weight. At most N lowering/raising steps occur, with direct validation, copying, arithmetic and sorting on lists of this size. The proposal's conservative quartic unary-work and quadratic auxiliary-bit bounds therefore suffice. Enumeration is verification code, not part of the map. No such bound has been established for the missing psi or complete endpoint composition.
