## A: inverse-first intrinsic column cores and increments

**Actual structural conjecture.** Every literal U has one canonical
C=(a,b,mu,nu) decomposition, with exact ascending nonnegative zero-padded
vectors, core weight a^2+3ab+3b^2 and increment weight |mu|+3|nu|.
The inverse reconstructs U from those coordinates by uniform parameter
rules. The F-height drops, including the terminal drop to zero, remain
strictly below five. No statistic is read from weight alone, and no
partition enumeration, ranking or hidden image table defines a map.

**Proved foothold and assurance.** Round one gives all a=0,1 and a=2 with
mu_1=0,1, arbitrary b and nu, explicit cores and intrinsic support/count
inverses. A nu suffix increment adds a literal3j column; a final mu increment
adds one; a merge/split gives a reversible cost-two operation. The separate
critic supported that universal restricted proof. Round two adds every
zero-pair a=2 case and mixed marker sectors mu_1 not4,5,9, with explicit
carries and inverse branches; the separate critic supported that proof too.
Thus the required mathematical foothold preceded extensive implementation.

The final author revision fills the three mixed holes, giving all a<=2
for arbitrary b,mu,nu, with written universal image-separation, validity,
weight and inverse arguments. Here a,b are recovered from marker species,
full/one-hole support, top counts and declared parity branches; nu comes
from residual multiplicities and reversed suffix sums, not a stored trace.
This final extension has not had separate semantic review. At weight30 it
covers70 of195 U objects, not all195. The 643 cumulative round trips in each
direction through30 are empirical implementation checks, not coverage proof.
See round-2-revision.md and partial-composition.md for exact rules/domains.

**Other intrinsic foothold.** On columns all divisible by three, ordinary
conjugation after scaling is a full classical correspondence to all
three-divisible gap partitions. Drop counts recover arbitrarily large a,b;
appending a3 is reversible and may change both counts. This provides a
natural global statistic on one infinite sector, but conflicts with some
marker assignments and cannot simply be united with that map. It is
classical, not a novelty claim. See three-divisible-foothold.md.

**Invariant and inverse.** Literal U validity, exact C weight and recovered
coordinate order. Forward and inverse are executable. The final partial
endpoint composition covers exactly all gap partitions with at most two
intrinsic singles and an input-recognizable residue subset.

**Decisive unproved lemma.** Extend or replace the decomposition with a

## Evidence-based selection and final learning

A was selected first for a proved inverse-first infinite family and explicit
reversible operation. Its carry obstruction led to revised restricted
formulas, not an impossibility claim. Later final-author work on B earned
attention because it produced genuine reversible source decompositions and
an intrinsic matched branch, rather than only coefficient agreement. The
original U–C complete-bijection route remains the leading objective; B is a
concrete competing proposal, with its missing match stated precisely.

collides on residues(1,8) and(3,6), both giving gap(3,6). Old assignments may
be replaced by a coherent full construction; they cannot all be preserved
