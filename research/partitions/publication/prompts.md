## Hero — new image, no reference

Use case: editorial mathematical art for Christopher Yamba / Yamba AI Math, a recreational mathematics feature titled 'The beauty of what remains'. Create one exquisite widescreen 3:2 landscape artwork, no text, no letters, no equations, no logos. A deep midnight indigo observatory space, very restrained nebula haze, arranged luminous translucent square tiles inspired by Young tableaux. On the left, paired rows of cool cyan and warm amber tiles approach each other and gently dissolve into fine particles; on the right, a small clean ascending constellation of violet and turquoise tiles remains, beautiful and legible. The visual metaphor is sign-reversing cancellation leaving positive survivors, not a literal mathematical diagram. Sophisticated mathematical magazine cover, sculptural frosted glass, precise edges, fine cinematic lighting, ample breathing space in upper left for a separately typeset title. Elegant, curious, luminous, contemplative; no noisy formula wallpaper, no people, no robots, no checker badges, no illegible symbols.

## Portrait cover — hero as style reference

Create a portrait 4:5 social-media cover in the same Christopher Yamba / Yamba AI Math visual language as the reference image: deep midnight cosmic space, precise luminous frosted glass square tiles in cyan, amber and violet, sophisticated mathematical magazine design. Use the reference as a style reference, not as a literal crop: compose anew for a vertical poster. In the lower two thirds, show two small paired rows of cyan and amber tiles dissolving gently into a stream of particles, leaving a small ordered group of violet and cyan square tiles on the lower right. This is conceptual artwork about cancellation and survivors, not an exact proof diagram. In the upper third, beautiful large readable warm-white editorial typography with exact title, on three lines: 'The beauty' / 'of cancellation'. Smaller subtitle below: 'A visual journey into a Lean-checked proof'. At the top, understated widely spaced small text: 'CHRISTOPHER YAMBA'. At the bottom, small text 'YAMBA AI MATH'. Very legible typography, generous margins, no additional words, no invented equations, no robots or people, no exaggerated proof seals or claims of a new theorem. Exceptional luminous mathematical art, crisp geometry and elegant restraint.

The supplied portrait is typeset in two large title lines and includes a final
period. Those harmless typographic variations were accepted on visual review.
