# B13 Novel contribution

yambench-canary-2a8022209f5b9b9c

Judge the single strongest new mathematical contribution in the inventory: a theorem, construction,
counterexample, obstruction, invariant or precisely stated conjecture with evidence. Compare it with the
closest work the run and the reading list actually inspected, and with the repository's own earlier
results. A restatement, a renaming or a routine specialization of known work is not new. A result that
adds hypotheses or restricts the problem's statement is judged as the restricted result it is.

| Anchor | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | Nothing new beyond known results, earlier repository results, or finite agreement with expectations. |
| 25 | A new, precisely stated conjecture or pattern with exact supporting evidence, not yet proved or refuted. |
| 50 | A new proved lemma, construction or witness of local interest, with separate review or a machine check. |
| 75 | A new proved result, construction or obstruction that changes the route to the target or extends known results, distinct from the closest inspected work. |
| 100 | A reviewed resolution of the target or of a recognized open subproblem, distinct from the closest inspected work. |

The engine caps this axis at 50 when the problem's closest work was not inspected at level T or higher.
Cite the artifact and the closest-work comparison you relied on. Score down one anchor when the run's
prose claims more novelty than its comparison supports.
