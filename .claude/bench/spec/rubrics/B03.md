# B03 Decisive finding

yambench-canary-57d790e42d41958c

Judge the single most decisive finding the run produced that is **not already a ladder milestone**.
Count only findings bound to a hashed artifact that you could recheck from the cited evidence.
The computed inventory lists candidates; it does not score them.

| Anchor | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | No finding beyond restating known results or finite agreement with expectations. |
| 25 | Finite exact evidence that replicates known behaviour or extends a checked range modestly. |
| 50 | A verified obstruction to, or refutation of, a proposed method, with a machine-checkable witness. |
| 75 | A verified obstruction that rules out a whole class of methods, or a new exact bound of independent interest. |
| 100 | A verified counterexample to the target statement, or a complete reviewed proof of a stated lemma on the critical path. |

Score down one anchor when the witness exists but its scope is overstated in the run's prose.
