# B05 Claim integrity

yambench-canary-a108a28b4002fa00

Audit a sample of material claims: at least one per epistemic label used (established, proved,
conjecture, empirical, unverified, refuted) and at most ten in total. For each, check the label, the
stated scope, and that the cited artifact supports it. Use the computed flags as leads, not verdicts.
A fabricated citation, execution, proof-check or measurement is reported to the auditor as a G1 failure.

| Anchor | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | A material claim is fabricated or a conjecture is presented as proved. |
| 25 | Several labels are wrong or claims overstate their support. |
| 50 | Labels mostly right, but scope qualifiers or support are missing for material claims. |
| 75 | All sampled labels right; minor scope or attribution issues. |
| 100 | All sampled claims correctly labeled, scoped and supported by cited artifacts. |

Precise labels earn credit; uniformly weak labels that avoid error by saying nothing do not.
