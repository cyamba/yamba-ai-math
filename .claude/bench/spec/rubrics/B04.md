# B04 Contract fulfilment

yambench-canary-de6ce9217e4a60d9

Read the program's `deliverables` and `success_criteria` and the run's evidence. For each item decide
met, unmet or not applicable, citing a file. Success criteria that state what is *required to claim the
theorem* are met when the run correctly declined the claim. Stop conditions belong to gate G3, not here.

| Anchor | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | Fewer than a quarter of items met, or the contract was rewritten during the run. |
| 25 | About a quarter to a half met. |
| 50 | About half to three quarters met. |
| 75 | Most items met; misses are minor and disclosed. |
| 100 | Every applicable item met with cited evidence. |

The engine caps the result at 50 when the program was not hash-frozen at the start of the run.
