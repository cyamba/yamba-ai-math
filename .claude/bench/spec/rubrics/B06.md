# B06 Adversarial review

yambench-canary-26f00ee083a667e7

For each review round, answer three items with true or false and cite the review file:

- `independent_check`: the reviewer ran or re-derived at least one check rather than only reading.
  A hand re-derivation of a witness counts; reading saved receipts does not.
- `objections_resolved`: every objection raised was resolved in the revision or preserved as an
  explicit disagreement.
- `no_coauthorship`: the reviewer did not author material it then reviewed.

A favourable verdict earns nothing by itself. A review that finds nothing because it checked nothing fails
`independent_check`.
