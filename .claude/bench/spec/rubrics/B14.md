# B14 Idea generation and route diversity

yambench-canary-ce749e49d7edc8fc

Count the distinct candidate ideas the run generated toward the problem: conjectures, constructions,
maps, invariants or proof routes. An idea counts only when its objects and claim are stated precisely and
it names a falsifier or decisive test. Two ideas are distinct when they rest on different mechanisms, not
when they vary a parameter of one mechanism. An idea is tested when it reached a verdict: a witness that
kills it, a proof or check that supports it, or a reviewed obstruction to its class.

| Anchor | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | No precisely stated idea beyond the starting route. |
| 25 | Two or more precise ideas, but from one mechanism or untested. |
| 50 | Three or more precise ideas from at least two mechanisms, at least one tested to a verdict. |
| 75 | Three or more mechanisms, most tested; at least one killed with a witness and at least one surviving with support. |
| 100 | As 75, and at least one generated idea became a proved result or a reviewed obstruction that redirected the work. |

Ideas listed without objects or falsifiers earn nothing. Killed ideas count only with their witness.
