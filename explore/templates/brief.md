# Replace with a precise research question

## Question

State the task, resource constraint, and comparison algorithm.

## Known foothold

Link primary sources beside the claims they support. Register inspection depth and date in `../../references/reading-list.md`.

## Proposed gap

**Novelty status: NOT VERIFIED.** Name the closest known construction and the exact proposed extension. Explain why the extension would matter even if its first theorem is narrow.

## Mathematical setup

Define inputs, outputs, distributions, symmetry or causal assumptions, precision, and what is observable. Separate representation assumptions from learnability assumptions.

## Unproved theorem targets

1. **Unproved:** State a finite proposition with explicit assumptions and a comparison class. Do not present a desired conclusion as a proved result.
2. **Unproved, optional:** A matching lower bound, robustness extension, or cost theorem.

## Counterexample or obstruction

Give a concrete way the proposed claim could fail, including missing information and misspecified priors.

## Minimal falsification experiment

Specify a small generator, a sweep of the controlling variable, matched-budget baselines, held-out tests, and a predeclared rejection criterion.

## Success and cost metrics

Include task utility, failure risk, coverage, training cost, inference latency, peak memory, external storage, precision, and verifier or tool cost where relevant.

## Next artifact

Name the first concrete deliverable: a lemma note, counterexample, source comparison, or runnable toy harness. Do not invent a completed result.
