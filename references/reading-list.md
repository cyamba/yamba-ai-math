## Inspection levels

- **A — Abstract/summary inspected:** primary abstract, landing-page summary, or introductory research summary. Full methods, theorem assumptions, proofs, and experimental details have **not** been audited. Some abstracts were delivered as primary-source search excerpts.
- **T — Theorem statement inspected:** the specified statement and surrounding assumptions were read in a primary full-paper excerpt. This is **not** a full proof audit or reproduction.
- **P — Full proof audited:** reserved for a recorded review of the complete proof and its dependencies. **No source currently has this level.**

The papers' conclusions are established prior-work footholds only within their actual assumptions. Every proposed extension in `explore/` remains unproved and **Novelty status: NOT VERIFIED**. No experiments were reproduced while assembling this ledger.

## Before promoting any seed

1. Read the closest full papers, including negative results and appendices; record version, theorem numbers, assumptions, and exact overlap.
2. Build an assumption matrix separating the existing theorem from the proposed extension. Retrieve later work and citations in both directions.
3. Audit the entire proof before assigning **P**. Record reproduced experiments separately; neither an abstract nor a clean proof establishes empirical usefulness.
4. Freeze the proposed claim, comparator, and resource boundary before testing. Preserve a negative outcome and distinguish it from an invalid experiment.
5. Update this ledger when a paper or source becomes inaccessible, is corrected, or changes version. The date above is a record of this inspection, not a permanent accuracy guarantee.
