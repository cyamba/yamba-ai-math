# Synthetic examples

`offline_worker.py` exercises the external-command protocol without model inference, source inspection, or mathematical research. Its responses explicitly identify themselves as synthetic fixtures. They can validate dependency delivery and result recording, not scientific performance.

`evaluations/champion.json` and `evaluations/challenger.json` are invented paired outcomes for demonstrating the comparison interface. Both have `synthetic: true` and are ineligible for a genuine improvement claim. They are not a benchmark, experiment, or evidence of a better harness.

See [the adapter contract](../docs/adapter.md) for real workers and [the meta scorecard](../meta/scorecard.md) for externally assessed research outcomes.
