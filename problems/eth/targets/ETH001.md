## Statement

Decide whether point-dependent measurement designs in the commuting swap algebra of the
openai/math family 378 method can beat the square root in its exponent. Use the cube polynomial
problem CP(Q, R) and the value $\beta$ (largest bad fraction over directions) as defined in
[SR-02](../subresults/SR-02-cube-polynomial-designs.md), for the uniform measure and for the
product measure $\mu_p$, $p=\frac{r-1}{2r}$. Completion requires one of:

1. **Construction.** For a fixed $C>4$ and infinitely many $R$, explicit families with
   $\beta\le R^{-C}$ at $Q\ge R^{(C+2)/3}$ (or any parameter sequence that satisfies the
   improvement criterion of [SR-04](../subresults/SR-04-slab-construction.md) with the cost account
   re-derived from the preprint's display (2.9) and constraints), with a general proof that the
   families are valid, exact verification of small members, a Lean-checked design lemma, and
   separate semantic review.
2. **Barrier.** A proof that for some fixed $\delta>0$ every family with $Q\ge R^{2+\delta}$ has
   $\beta\ge R^{-4-o(1)}$, with separate semantic review. Lean certification of its
   combinatorial core is a subsequent obligation.

Either outcome concerns the method only; it does not decide whether the preprint's theorem is
true. **Novelty status: NOT VERIFIED.**

## Scope deltas

A result that restricts the class of designs (region-based designs, fixed node sets, $R=2$), the
range (finite $Q$, fixed ratio $Q/R$), the measure (uniform only) or the objective (average
instead of maximum over directions) is `restricted` relative to this target and lists each
difference as its scope delta. Exact data on small cases are `empirical` and never complete the
target.
