## Statement

Decide whether measurement designs in the commuting swap algebra of the openai/math family 378
method, with node-dependent or single measurement matrices, can beat the square root in its
exponent. (Designs of the preprint's own template cannot: SR-01.) Use the cube polynomial
problem CP(Q, R) and the value $\beta$ (largest bad fraction over directions) as defined in
[SR-02](../subresults/SR-02-cube-polynomial-designs.md), for the uniform measure and for the
product measure $\mu_p$, $p=\frac{r-1}{2r}$. Completion requires one of:

1. **Construction.** For a fixed $C>3$ and infinitely many $R$, explicit families with
   $\beta\le R^{-C}$ at $Q\approx R^{(C+1)/2}$ (with zero measurement rows dropped, as Definition 3.1
   allows; $C>4$ at $Q\approx R^{(C+2)/3}$ under the preprint's own row count), or any parameter
   sequence that satisfies the improvement criterion of
   [SR-04](../subresults/SR-04-slab-construction.md) with the cost account re-derived from the
   preprint's display (2.11) and constraints (2.17), with a general proof that the
   families are valid, exact verification of small members, a Lean-checked design lemma, and
   separate semantic review.
2. **Barrier.** A proof that, within the preprint's cost accounting (display (2.11) and its
   constraints, with zero rows dropped), no point-dependent swap design achieves exponent per variable
   $\lambda^{-1/2-\varepsilon}$ for a fixed $\varepsilon>0$, $\lambda=\log_2n$, with separate semantic
   review. Sufficient, for example: every family with $Q\ge R^{2+\delta}$ has $\beta\ge R^{-3-o(1)}$
   ($R^{-4-o(1)}$ under the preprint's own count); or the touched-set conjecture of
   [SR-10](../subresults/SR-10-touched-vertices-barrier-criterion.md) in the form $\mu_p(W)\ge c/R$ for
   every family with $R\le c_0\sqrt Q$ (the form $c/R^2$ suffices under the preprint's own
   count). Lean certification of the combinatorial core is a subsequent obligation.

Either outcome concerns the method only; it does not decide whether the preprint's theorem is
true. **Novelty status: NOT VERIFIED.**
