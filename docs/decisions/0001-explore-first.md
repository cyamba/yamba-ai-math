# Decision 0001: Explore first; keep the harness accountable

- Date: 2026-10-20
- Status: accepted project scope
- Context: the project seeks mathematical ideas for compact, capable, reliable AI, together with an agentic research harness that can assemble specialized teams.

## Decision

Begin with research-angle Markdown files in `explore/`, a reusable agent and fleet vocabulary, bounded investigation programs, and a small execution interface. Keep ideas about neural systems and evidence about harness effectiveness in distinct records. Support both manual investigation and operator-configured external execution.

Use stage gates so exploratory creativity can coexist with precise evidence. All starting briefs are seeds, and all proposed new theorem targets remain unproved unless an explicit subsequent record establishes otherwise. No initial harness benchmark or optimization result is claimed.

Treat self-improvement as controlled experimentation: bounded proposals, fixed evaluations, reviewer checks, a change ledger, and reversible promotion. The harness may propose changes to its own methods; it cannot grant itself expanded goals, spending, permissions, or revised standards.

## Consequences

The initial product is a usable research workspace and orchestration scaffold. It is not a trained model or an autonomous discovery claim. Later work can promote strong ideas into articles, libraries, models, and larger research programs when evidence justifies that investment.
