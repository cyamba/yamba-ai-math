# Topic title

**Status.** One line: the claim's status, who or what produced it, what checked it, and the
source link.

## In one sentence

The key idea, as a mechanism rather than a conclusion.

## Picture

One figure with a caption that says what to notice. Generator: `figures/make_<name>.py`.

## Smallest example

The smallest nontrivial case, fully worked. Computed by `examples.py` (range checked: ...).

## Pattern

A few more examples or a table, and the regularity in words.

## Precise statement

Definitions and the statement, with every quantifier and hypothesis. One non-example per
hypothesis, showing what fails.

## Why it is true

The idea and a skeleton of three to seven moves. Heuristics labeled as heuristics.

## Step by step

The argument or algorithm with a justification tag on each step, or a guided reading of the
formal proof. Mark any GAP.

## How to use it

Hypothesis checklist, recognition cues, one worked application, common misuses.

## Questions it raises

Generalizations, converses, constructive versions, each labeled as a question or a conjecture,
with where it was routed.

## Sources

Primary sources with dates checked.
