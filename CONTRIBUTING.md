# Contributing

Start with a narrow mathematical question or an observed harness failure. Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md), the [research protocol](docs/research-protocol.md), and any local instructions before editing.

For a research angle, copy [the brief template](explore/templates/brief.md), assign an unused `E` identifier, and update the index. Include primary references, assumptions, an explicitly unproved target, an obstruction, and a decisive next experiment. Check close prior work before claiming novelty. Record negative results and counterexamples as carefully as improvements.

For a harness change, explain the failure it addresses and follow [the meta protocol](meta/protocol.md) when claiming an improvement in research outcomes. Keep executable correctness tests separate from claims about scientific usefulness. Run the checks listed in the README. Include resource boundaries and enough information to repeat any reported experiment.

Do not submit invented citations, generated benchmark results presented as observations, unchecked proof claims, secrets, or third-party material without appropriate rights. A model-generated draft is welcome when its material claims are checked and its evidence status is clear.

Keep pull requests reviewable: state the concrete question or problem, what changed, how it was checked, and what remains uncertain. Publication and model training are later research activities, not automatic consequences of adding an idea.
