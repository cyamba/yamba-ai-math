# KR1 small-contribution intake

User-requested, 27 October 2026. This is a documentation-only intake convention;
it changes no harness code, active research contract, proof standard or resource
authorization. Workflow benefits are unvalidated. Keep the complete KR1 objective
in [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md) and the current investigation's next obligation.

Keep mathematical artifacts in their existing research folders. Register incoming
work in the [contribution ledger](../research/rogers-ramanujan/contributions.md)
with a stable ID and links; do not copy proofs or rewrite historical results.

## Intake and disposition

| Stage | Required record |
| --- | --- |
| Inbox | Original claim or observation, attribution and source provenance |
| Triage | Exact scope, closest existing result and named global obligation |
| Verification | Written argument or reproducible falsifier; computation bounds separately |
| Separate review | Linked verdict, hypotheses, unresolved objections and assurance level |
| Integration | Compatibility proof and exactly which existing obligation is closed |

Record assurance separately from disposition: **integrated**, **supporting**,
**parked** or **refuted**. A reviewed local theorem may remain supporting.
Counterexamples can close an obstruction obligation without defining a new map.
An incomplete candidate stays inbox/triage; do not promote it by agreement alone.

## Contribution card

Record ID/date, attribution, exact statement/domain, target obligation and
dependencies, prior-work overlap, proof/code/evidence/review links, disposition,
significance and precise next action. For a proposed bijection, include its
source/target, forward and inverse rules, weight, termination, both inverse
identities and exact image/coverage claim. State novelty and formal assurance.

Integration requires a written argument for source-domain compatibility,
disjoint images or coherent rerouting of existing assignments, intrinsic inverse
branch recovery, and the exact coverage gained and still missing. Attach separate
semantic review to new substantive integration claims. A generating function,
finite agreement or several valid subfamilies alone does not supply this argument.

## Keep intake small

Triage ends with a disposition and next obligation. Supporting/parked entries
start no investigation, dispatch, new dependency, push or publication. Further
work follows its own authorized contract. Reuse existing proofs and assurance;
do not reopen closed runs or replace the main handoff with the intake ledger.

End research updates with **Significance** relative to the main objective and a
**Progress metric** naming obligations actually closed, integration status and
verification scope. Separate supporting-lemma counts from global proof progress;
do not infer a completion percentage from counts or finite coverage.
